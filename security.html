Security at FEECENT

Your security is our top priority

Multi-Layer Security

We employ multiple layers of security to protect your money and personal information.

🔐 Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption - the same standard used by major banks worldwide.

👤 Biometric Authentication

Log in with your face or fingerprint. Biometric data never leaves your device and is never stored on our servers.

📱 Single Session Policy

Your account can only be active on one device at a time. If you log in on a new device, the previous session is immediately terminated.

🔔 Real-time Alerts

Get instant notifications for all transactions. You'll know immediately if any activity occurs on your account.

🛡️ Fraud Monitoring

Our AI-powered fraud detection system monitors all transactions 24/7 for suspicious activity.

Security Tips

  • Never share your PIN or passcode with anyone
  • Enable two-factor authentication for your email account
  • Only download the FEECENT app from official app stores
  • Keep your device's operating system updated
  • Report any suspicious activity immediately

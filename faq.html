Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I open a FEECENT account?

Download the FEECENT app from Google Play or App Store, tap "Create Account", and follow the registration steps. You'll need a valid email address and phone number.

Is FEECENT a bank?

No. FEECENT is a bill payment and money transfer app, not a bank. We don't hold a banking license. Wallet, virtual account, and money transfer services on FEECENT are powered by our licensed payment technology partner, and your funds are held with that partner's licensed financial institutions, not with FEECENT directly.

Is FEECENT safe?

Yes! We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption, biometric authentication, and real-time fraud monitoring to keep your money safe.

How long do transfers take?

Internal transfers are instant. External transfers to other banks take 1-5 minutes for same bank transfers and up to 30 minutes for interbank transfers.

What are the savings plans?

We offer Harvest Plan (save daily, get food items), Fixed Savings (30-day lock, 5% interest), SaveBox (flexible savings with early withdrawal option), and Target Savings (save toward a specific goal).

How do I upgrade my account tier?

Go to Settings → Upgrade Account. For Tier 2, submit your ID (NIN/BVN). For Tier 3, submit proof of address after email verification.

What if I forget my passcode?

Use the "Forgot Passcode" option on the login screen. You'll need to verify your identity with an OTP sent to your email.

Can I use FEECENT on multiple devices?

For security, your account can only be active on one device at a time. Logging into a new device will automatically log you out of the previous one.

How do I contact support?

You can use live chat in the app, email support@feecent.online, or call +234 816 630 1669 (Mon-Fri, 9AM-6PM).
